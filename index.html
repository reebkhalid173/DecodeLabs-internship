Who We Are

Structure first. Design follows.

Apex Digital is a boutique web studio founded on a simple belief: great websites are built on solid foundations. We prioritize information architecture and semantic structure before diving into visual design.

Over the past five years, we've shipped more than 120 production websites for clients in e-commerce, SaaS, and creative industries. Every project undergoes our rigorous quality assurance process, ensuring W3C validation, semantic correctness, and a Lighthouse score of 90 or above.

  • ◆
    Clean HTML Semantic, accessible, and machine-readable markup.
  • ◆
    Modular CSS Reusable, maintainable, and DRY stylesheets.
  • ◆
    Accessibility WCAG AA compliant with keyboard navigation.

Why Choose Us

Quality isn't optional — it's our standard.

Every project must pass our three-stage quality checkpoint: W3C validation, semantic audit, and visual regression testing.

✓ Zero Validation Errors
Every HTML file is validated before delivery. No missing attributes, no deprecated elements, no syntax errors.
✓ Semantic Structure
Proper use of header, main, nav, and section landmarks. One H1 per page, no unnecessary div wrappers.
